This is a community-modified NVIDIA runtime retargeted for Ada / RTX 40
hardware. Its embedded NVIDIA Authenticode signature does not validate after
the community modification. It is not official NVIDIA RTX 40 support and is
loaded only when DLSS5RTX40Experimental is explicitly enabled. The original
RTX 50 runtime remains separate and unchanged. If initialization or evaluation
fails, Zink disables only Neural Rendering and continues through ordinary DLSS.
