- Intel XeSS/XeLL runtime files remain subject to Intel's applicable SDK/runtime terms.
- NVIDIA Streamline, NGX, DLSS, DLSS-G, Reflex and related runtime files remain subject to NVIDIA's applicable terms and the notices included with the package.
- The DirectML/OptiScaler experiment retains its upstream notices and licences.
- fxReloader retains its GPL notice and corresponding-source archive in the packaged backend.
- NaturalVision Evolved is not part of this repository or release archive. Users must obtain it from its authorized publisher.
- Grand Theft Auto V and related trademarks belong to Rockstar Games/Take-Two Interactive. This is an unofficial community project.

Review the notice and licence files in `Backend` and `Source/Mod` before redistributing a build.
